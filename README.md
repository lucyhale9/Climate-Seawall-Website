# How High Should the Seawall Be?

An interactive website for **ATMO 215: Climate Change in the 21st Century**. A coastal city has to choose how tall to build a seawall that will last until 2100. Sea level rise acts as a **rising baseline under storm surge**: as the sea rises, the same storm reaches higher, so a wall that is safe today gets overtopped more and more often over its lifetime.

Use the sliders for **wall height**, **sea level rise by 2100**, and **today's 100-year storm surge**. The live graph shows the chance per year that the wall is overtopped from today to 2100, and the big number shows the chance of at least one overtopping over the wall's lifetime.

**Live site:** https://lucyhale9.github.io/Climate-Seawall-Website/
